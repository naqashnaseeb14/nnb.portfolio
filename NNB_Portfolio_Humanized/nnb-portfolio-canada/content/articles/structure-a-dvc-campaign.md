dek: A good launch needs more than a film and a release date. Here is how I plan the content around it.

category: Campaign planning
intro: When a client is releasing a brand film, the first question is not just when to post it. I want
  to know who needs to see it, what should happen before it goes live and what we will share afterwards.
sections:
- heading: Start with the people you want to reach
  body: Before making the calendar, I ask a few basic questions. Who is this for? What do they already
    know? What would make them stop and watch? What should they do after seeing the film?
- heading: Give each stage a purpose
  body: Teasers can build curiosity. Launch day is when the full story becomes clear. Follow-up edits
    give people something useful to share, remember or ask about after the main release.
- heading: Make the team’s job easier
  body: I prepare the briefs, dates and asset list early. Designers, editors and advertising specialists
    should know what they are delivering, who approves it and when it needs to be ready.
- heading: Look at what happened, then adjust
  body: Once content is live, I check the numbers in context. Views tell us something about distribution.
    Comments, enquiries and conversions tell us other things. The next step depends on what we actually
    learn.
