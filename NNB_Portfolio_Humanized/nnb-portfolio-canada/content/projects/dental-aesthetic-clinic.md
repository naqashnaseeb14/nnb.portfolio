summary: I restarted regular posting for a dental and aesthetic clinic, planned the content and worked through a
  careful approval process.
role: I researched the market, planned the posts, wrote captions, managed publishing and replies, and helped direct
  the videos.
challenge: The clinic’s pages had been inactive for nearly a year. The content covered dental and aesthetic treatments,
  so every post needed extra care and sign-off before publication.
insight: When a business works in a sensitive field, it is better to plan approvals in advance than rush content
  through at the last minute.
strategy:
- Look at how other dental and aesthetic clinics explain their services.
- Plan different post formats for the clinic’s two service areas.
- Leave time for reviews and approvals before scheduling anything.
execution:
- label: Research
  text: I looked at local and international clinic content before deciding on the topics and tone.
- label: Video content
  text: I helped create a doctor introduction video that included a client testimonial. We then used the same format
    for two more videos.
- label: Approvals
  text: One offer needed four rounds of feedback. I kept track of the changes and waited for sign-off before publishing.
result: The supplied report shows 16,854 organic Instagram views, around 2,600 accounts reached and 222 interactions
  between 6 November and 3 December 2025. Facebook recorded 937 views and 254 viewers. These figures do not tell
  us how many patients booked an appointment.
reflection: The most useful lesson was to plan enough time for reviews. Even with several rounds of changes, we
  could keep the content moving without ignoring important approvals.
