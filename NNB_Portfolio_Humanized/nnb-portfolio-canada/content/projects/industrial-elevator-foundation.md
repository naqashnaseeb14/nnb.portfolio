summary: I set up the accounts, built a content plan and managed an ad campaign that reported 65 inbound calls.
  The call quality and sales impact have not been verified.
role: I set up the social accounts, planned the content and creatives, wrote the copy and ran the Meta campaign.
challenge: The company had no social media accounts or existing content to work with. I needed to find a way to
  show what the business actually did and reach people involved in construction and property development.
insight: Real installations make better evidence than broad promises. Showing an elevator already installed in a
  building gives potential buyers something concrete to assess.
strategy:
- Set up the social accounts properly before publishing.
- Look at how other industrial businesses explain technical products and adapt the ideas for this brand.
- Show a real installed elevator in the ad so potential customers can see an actual example of the work.
execution:
- label: Getting started
  text: I created the accounts, secured them with two-step verification and completed the profiles.
- label: Organic content
  text: I planned the content and directed the creatives. The client later reported appearing among the top search
    results for its name, although that ranking has not been independently checked.
- label: Meta campaign
  text: I wrote the ad, planned the audience and ran a campaign aimed at decision makers in Islamabad, Rawalpindi,
    Gujranwala and Lahore.
result: The client portfolio reports 65 inbound calls at PKR 276 per call. The account reports also show 5,374 organic
  Instagram views in November to December, 2,539 in December to January, and 5,406 organic Facebook views in December
  to January. The calls have not been verified as qualified leads or sales. Those are different outcomes.
reflection: This was a good example of starting with very little and building a usable content and advertising plan.
  The reported calls are encouraging, but they should not be confused with confirmed orders.
