summary: A practical content plan for Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and LinkedIn, without turning every

role: I handled social strategy, content planning, captions, publishing and community responses across
  four platforms.
challenge: The business needed to stay visible online, but a feed full of property listings alone would
  not give people many reasons to follow it. The plan also had to work across four different platforms.
insight: Someone considering a property needs confidence in the people behind it. Useful information and
  a consistent presence can help build that confidence over time.
strategy:
- Build a manageable schedule of roughly 10 to 15 posts per month.
- Adapt the message and format to each platform instead of posting the exact same thing everywhere.
- Balance property content with posts that help people understand the brand and its work.
execution:
- label: Content direction
  text: I chose content themes that could build familiarity and trust rather than focusing entirely on
    listings.
- label: Calendar and captions
  text: I planned roughly 10 to 15 posts a month, with copy suited to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and
    LinkedIn.
- label: Publishing and replies
  text: I published posts and managed community responses across the four channels.
- label: Checking progress
  text: I used platform data to see what people responded to. I did not count views or comments as property
    visits or sales.
result: A supplied 90-day snapshot reports 9,822 Facebook views (+45.4%), 554 content interactions (+129.9%)
  and 3,152 viewers (+45.9%). These are platform engagement indicators, not qualified buyer enquiries,
  site visits or sales. Exact snapshot dates and exports must be verified before public use.
reflection: A steady content plan can be more useful than posting constantly. The next step is to connect
  social activity more closely to genuine buyer enquiries and visits.
