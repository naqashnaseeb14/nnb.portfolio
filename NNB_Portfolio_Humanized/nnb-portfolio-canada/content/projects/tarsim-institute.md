summary: A working plan for student-facing content, creative briefs, video releases and paid-media handoffs.

role: Content strategy, calendars, creative briefs, publishing plans and coordination with designers,
  editors and the media buyer. The specialist manages the ads.
challenge: Students need clear answers about programmes, eligibility and how to apply. Different pieces
  of content are needed for awareness, consideration and admission enquiries, and everyone involved needs
  the same schedule.
insight: A student watching a first introduction has different questions from someone ready to speak to
  an admissions counsellor. The content should reflect that.
strategy:
- Plan messages around the questions students ask at each stage.
- Make programme and eligibility information easy to understand.
- Schedule videos, static creatives and follow-up posts around important admissions dates.
- Give designers, editors and the media buyer clear briefs and deadlines.
execution:
- label: Content plan
  text: Organize the calendar around programme awareness, eligibility, common questions and the application
    process.
- label: Creative briefs
  text: Prepare briefs for designers and editors, collect feedback and get the approved files ready for
    publishing.
- label: Video releases
  text: Plan when teasers, the main film and shorter follow-up edits should go out, where those assets
    are part of the approved campaign.
- label: Paid ads handoff
  text: Give the advertising specialist the audience, objective, creative options and reporting questions.
    The specialist runs the ads.
- label: Admissions reporting
  text: Keep form submissions separate from eligible enquiries, counselling appointments and confirmed
    enrolments.
result: This campaign is still in progress. No admission totals or cost-per-enrolment figures have been
  cleared for publication. Any targets are planning goals, not completed results.
reflection: If the creative, ads and admissions team work from different plans, promising enquiries can
  get lost. A shared calendar and clear handoffs make the process easier to manage.
